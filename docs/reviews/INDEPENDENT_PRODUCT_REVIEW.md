# Independent Product and UX Integration Review

**Purpose:** Challenge the integrated architecture, financial/research plans and backlog from a product/UX perspective independent of their authors.

**Status:** DRAFT — review of design documents, not an implementation test or Design Gate 1 approval.

## Scope and review method

Reviewed `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `RISK_MODEL.md`, `AI_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md`, `SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `TEST_STRATEGY.md`, `UAT_PLAN.md`, `IMPLEMENTATION_BACKLOG.md` and ADR-0001 through ADR-0010. Compared them with product/requirements/UX documents, looking specifically for promised capabilities that cannot follow from the proposed service states or acceptance tests. The reviewer authored the product/UX requirements but did not author the architecture, risk, AI, security, experiment, test/UAT or backlog documents being independently challenged.

No application tests or broker checks were executed: this phase is design only. Source limitations and qualification gates are not treated as working safeguards.

## Final fourteen-question challenge

| Question | Review answer and remaining evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Could an unauthorized real-money trade occur? | No live capability is authorized or added by this design phase. Proposed V0 relies on absent live adapter/routes, paper-only credential provenance and egress denial, all still to be tested. Compromised host/execution remains a residual threat; design prose is not proof. |
| 2. Could tenants access each other's information? | Application/RLS/composite-key/job/cache/export controls are specified, but IP-06 must be resolved and tested at the database boundary before tenant data. Tenant IDs alone are not authority. |
| 3. Could compromised Internet research trade directly? | The proposed network, identity and tool boundaries deny direct access. Indirect poisoning can still create a plausible bounded proposal, so factual validation and outcomes remain necessary. |
| 4. Could an LLM obtain broker credentials? | Allowlisted projections, execution-only custody and no secret-manager/model route are specified. Actual container reachability, environment/mount and canary tests are required; same-host root compromise is not contained. |
| 5. Could ambiguous requests duplicate trades? | Stable intent IDs, one outstanding account intent, retained reservations and no blind resend address ambiguity. Fault-injection must prove worker/reboot/timeouts; no exactly-once remote guarantee is claimed. |
| 6. Can every financial action be reconstructed? | The proposed audit/ledger/provenance chain is sufficient conceptually. Licensed-source retention can limit reconstruction of original text; local hash chains do not defeat full host rewriting. Restore/checkpoint evidence and honest limits are mandatory. |
| 7. Are unnecessary costs required? | No mandatory paid service. External subscription allocation and missing costs are counted; one-host operational effort remains real though outside incremental compute cost. Keep many-service/event-bus expansion deferred. |
| 8. Can the local Ubuntu development host run it? | Architecture is plausible for measured small workloads; exact RAM/disk/GPU/identity/TDE compatibility has not been inventoried or benchmarked. IP-08 prevents a claim of proven fit. |
| 9. Can commercial multi-tenancy evolve without a rewrite? | Tenant ownership, scoped contracts and isolated execution offer a credible path. Commercial support/privacy/legal/HA/isolation gates still require new operational work; portability is not a commercial-readiness claim. |
| 10. Does it measure AI value instead of assuming it? | A/B/C/D, preregistration, costs, rejected ideas, regimes, uncertainty and negative results are explicit. Preserve IP-09 and avoid interpreting a profitable short pilot as edge. |
| 11. Are quantitative tasks separated from LLM tasks? | Allocation assigns arithmetic, screening, exposure, risk and accounting to deterministic/statistical processing; language models interpret and challenge evidence. Ablations should remove roles that add no value. |
| 12. Can humans understand proposals and risk decisions? | Dedicated evidence/bull-bear/provenance/reason views distinguish proposal, preview, final risk, dispatch and fill. IP-02/04 corrections align the critical UX. Mobile/assistive UAT remains required. |
| 13. Are outages covered? | Host/DB/OpenBao/broker/model/data/network/NAS failures have safe-state and recovery proposals. Cancellation is best effort; stop acknowledgement does not promise no residual fills. Test operator-unavailable and disk-full recovery. |
| 14. What complexity can be removed? | Keep one model job, logical roles, DB outbox, modular core, explicit trust boundaries, human P0 dispatch and one broker-linked portfolio/account. Defer OAuth, paid APIs, NATS/Redis, billing, public signup and many microservices. |

## Review conclusion

The documents form a coherent proposal after the listed integration corrections, not an approved or proven system. IP-01/IP-02/IP-03 corrections were independently reread, and `git diff --check` passed during this review. The final architecture also explicitly limits local fencing: an expired lease never permits another outbound attempt, and a paused old worker plus takeover is a required fault test. Retain IP-06/IP-07/IP-08 as explicit human/qualification gates with accountable owners. No requirement should silently relax to achieve a successful paper order. Human Design Gate 1 review remains necessary before implementation.
